## Initialization and limits

The wake test exercised the explicit sensor power-up/soft-reset interval before
patch loading. It also exercised the logged normalization of undocumented
configuration byte `0x2c` (`0x69` → `0x01` on this wake). That byte is retained
as actual metadata but exempted from exact comparison; the remaining page,
including every requested setting, must match readback. Application patch,
identity and configuration checks succeeded after wake.

Synchronization acceptance is the conservative **software timing model** in
[ADR-0003](../../Design/ADR/0003-synchronized-rgb-depth-bursts.md). The host checks
the timing evidence independently; this is not an external optical strobe
calibration or hardware-triggered simultaneous exposure test. The mounted indoor
scene does not validate absolute 11 m accuracy or geometric RGB/depth alignment.

The 10 seconds is actual deep-sleep timer duration. Sensor/camera warmup, SD
setup, checked shutdown and diagnostic grace add cycle overhead. Only selected
exposures within the 30-second recording window are saved; finishing an SD write
can extend the solid LED interval slightly. `camera_off` means driver/XCLK
shutdown, not removal of its power rail. Current consumption, motion wake and
power-loss recovery testing remain later work.

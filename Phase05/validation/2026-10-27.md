# Phase05 hardware validation — 2026-10-27

**Passed:** independent nominal 10 fps long-range 8×8 depth and 1 fps QXGA RGB,
two 30-second bursts, 10-second deep-sleep timer wake, full depth readback for both
bursts and full JPEG readback for the first. Automatic cycling was restored.

## Iteration-budget measurement

Before selecting the shipping profile, a sensor probe ran alongside the QXGA
camera with the long-range preset, **2000 kilo-iterations** and a requested
100 ms period. It received 24 results; discarding the first four gave a mean
**189,368 µs/frame**, approximately **5.28 fps**. Two consecutive probe runs
reported the same rounded mean. Raw diagnostics are retained under
`validation/local/iterations2000-probe.log`.

The requested period does not override a longer physical acquisition. Phase05
therefore defaults to **1000k**, preserving nominal 10 fps and the manufacturer's
long-range optical settings. Startup independently measures cadence before each
burst and rejects a mean outside 95–105 ms. This is an explicit change from the
suggested 2000k preference, required to meet the requested rate.

## Practical limits

- **1000k is the validated profile.** 2000k did not sustain 10 fps. Configuring a
  long-range preset does not establish physical 11 m performance in this indoor
  scene; target reflectivity, illumination and integration remain relevant.
- Timestamps and conservative intervals support offline association. The reported
  nearest-midpoint differences are not externally calibrated shutter offsets or a
  guarantee that all exposures are within 50 ms. Geometric alignment is separate.
- Depth lives in a bounded PSRAM archive during capture and becomes durable only
  at end-of-burst commit. Interruption before that commit loses the depth archive.
  The capture LED is low during final SD writes.
- **10 seconds is actual ESP deep-sleep timer duration.** The measured ~4.4-second
  archive commit, diagnostic grace and sensor/camera startup add to total off time.
  Current consumption and motion wake were not measured in this phase.

See [ADR-0004](../../Design/ADR/0004-independent-depth-rgb-bursts.md) for timing,
buffer ownership, file layout and the versioned binary format.
